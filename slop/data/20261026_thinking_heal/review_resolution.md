## Settled generic filter

Before any training, exclude a trace whose native reasoning has **8 or more case-insensitive whole-word occurrences of `wait`**. This conservative repeated-indecision screen was chosen after reading the pilot, as the supervisor requested. It is a style-defect screen, not a proof that other traces do not loop. All flagged IDs and exact counts go into `build_manifest.json`; originals remain in `raw.jsonl` and full-read batches. Full semantic reads can identify additional loops or factual issues that the lexical screen misses. No automatic correctness acceptance is inferred from finish=stop.

All 600 selected prompt sides were read in full. They are ordinary arithmetic, coding, extraction and summarization, not moral-dilemma or identity questions. Some source texts mention history, wrongdoing, religion or fiction; the requested operation is still neutral extraction/arithmetic, not a character judgment. Existing ambiguous GSM seeds, impossible calendar dates in ordinary invitation seeds, and risky medication arithmetic are flagged for full-read reviewers, not silently rewritten. The remaining full response reads are mandatory before training.

The parent approved preparation quarantine and 6+30 fixed full response reads, not approval to train the unread remainder. `pilot_read.json` authorizes *generation only* after this resolution, not training or release.
