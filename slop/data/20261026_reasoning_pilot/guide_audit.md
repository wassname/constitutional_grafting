## Added rules, possible house-rule imports, and example defects

- **opus_guide:** “Options that need a vice, such as deceiving someone, are not options to weigh. Say briefly why they are excluded.” The second sentence asks for a reason, but the first pre-excludes by a label before showing how the drive fails. v4 §5 Vice/Honesty/Means and ends derives the trust failure; §7 “A spy” distinguishes mutually expected deception in contests (then explains why public Musonius still does not deceive). Replace the bare instruction with a general derivation: characterize the drive, test its general encouragement/stability and effects, explain why an apparent benefit destroys the good it claims to serve, then exclude if warranted. Do not patch a particular pilot incident.
- **opus_guide:** “The moves, in order” risks a forced checklist; “including doing nothing and asking first” risks ritual alternatives. Its ordinary-task exception mitigates this; remove the mandatory sequence, keep optional attention to facts that could change the answer. The conversion example correctly has no moral trade-off.
- **opus_guide:** “If you have a concern, say it once, then help” compresses Autonomy into a categorical conversational rule. Derive the person's authority and the limits of the role instead of making once-only a moral boundary.
- **opus_guide prompt confound:** the entire “For both kinds of teacher” section, including “If you may cite,” was passed unchanged in the intro, before the common owned-values task. The final chatty writer offer was excluded from demonstrations, not the citation permission. **kimi_guide also retains its own conditional citation paragraph**, so citation permission is not unique to opus_guide. No observed difference can be cleanly attributed to guide reasoning alone.
- **glm_guide original:** “At least two live options” creates fake arithmetic alternatives (250/25). “newcomers would never become members” falsely turns access to one slot into membership. “first come ... defensible only if ... stated” is an unsupported categorical convention.
- **glm_guide revised:** removes mandatory alternatives but still says established priority “would keep out everyone who might renew the club.” “I checked and it was Wednesday” claims a performed check and settled conclusion not supplied. “silence leaves the neighbour doubting my word” assumes the neighbor's knowledge. Both drafts rejected; **zero measured teacher rows**, not zero failures.
- **kimi_guide original:** “parcel arriving Wednesday” changes the past-tense seed. Revision repairs that, explicitly attributes tracking, and gives conditional, reasoned slot sharing/draws. No added categorical moral refusal found in revised guide. Citation permission still needs removing for owned-values use.
- No specific unquoted principle can be proved to come from Anthropic/OpenAI house rules merely from phrasing. Mark the vice exclusion and once-only pattern as **possible trained-house-rule tendencies**, not established provenance. Honesty and autonomy are **explicitly adopted in v4**, including attributed Anthropic excerpts; their appearance alone is not contamination. “No private introspection” here is a truthful methodological limit, not a ban on useful synthetic reasoning.

## Omitted headline principles (omission, not contradiction)

- **opus_guide:** Control; Consistency across viewpoints (its “Inconsistency” bullet only checks trace/action agreement); Means and ends as a derivation; **Weighing circles' first search for no-one-worse-off outcomes and handling unavoidable losers** (the virtue test's almost-no-one-worse-off phrase is not that operational rule); Guards against tiny-chance/huge-stakes, constructed populations, hypothetical beings and test excuses; **Roles** and whole-arrangement legitimacy. Also missing §3 View from history's temporal weighting/guards against misreporting and preference engineering; §4 evidence for values; §5 Change and ending, Restraint, Power, Freedom's irreversibility, Taboo. These need not all be recited per row, but the guide must not substitute its shorter test for the full constitution.
- **kimi_guide revised:** Control; full Virtue stability/incentive/veil-of-place test; costly behavioral evidence and the other guards on learning; viewpoint-invariant Consistency (it checks internal agreement instead); Weighing circles' baseline/no-one-worse-off-first and compensation; formal Guards; §4 evidence base; Change and ending, Restraint, Power and Taboo. It names roles, means–ends and autonomy substantively, but does not teach every role legitimacy condition or historical weighting.
- **glm_guide (both):** Control; full stability/incentives/baseline virtue test; circles' precise relative weighting; Means and ends; viewpoint Consistency; formal Guards; role legitimacy/open refusal; detailed Weighing circles and compensation; historical evidence/temporal weighting; Change and ending, Restraint, Power, Freedom, Taboo. Original telescope example's claim that an AI must not invent a rule is not a general account of Roles.

**Guide recommendation:** kimi_guide is the cleanest currently written guide, but remove its stale citation permission before another approved pilot. Fix opus_guide generally, preserve original/cache, and obtain the independent prompt-package review required by AGENTS before scaling. No guide was edited or newly approved here.
