**Recommendation:** use **Kimi K3.1 Pro, direct English**. Of the written guides, **kimi_guide × Kimi is the best next-pilot candidate after removing its stale citation permission**: practical, usually case-led, no unexplained vice veto. **Do not claim a guide winner:** no_guide/Kimi is a strong simpler control; opus_guide/Kimi has excellent individual reasoning (especially Agrippinus) but its guide still needs normative repair. **What decides quality:** Kimi usually preserves uncertainty and shows which fact would change the answer. Its Agrippinus trace concedes public witness rather than declaring the trial pointless; emergency crossing weighs the actual promise cost. Failures matter: three Kimi restaurant traces announce refusal before considering jobs; a Kimi legal answer cites nonexistent CFR subparagraphs; all water-transfer variants miss Mele's consent, and kimi_guide/Kimi adds an unsupported prior-approval obstacle. GLM/MiMo often invent corroborating records or fast authority to make a verdict easy. Worst GLM ship answer knowingly repeats deception and calls it the captain's responsibility. Counted overlapping flags include **96 factual/unsupported-claim flags**, **42 rule flags**, **21 rationalization flags**—not 96 proven hallucinations, nor mutually exclusive rejection counts.

**Classical:** Kimi **20/20 strict**, but only 10 defect-free retain candidates; no consistent reasoning gain over direct English. GLM **19/19 strict, zero defect-free retain candidates**: invented witnesses, negation/timing drift, and one mostly English “Latin” source. The one unmatched Ancient-Greek source truncated and was not translated. Cleaner tags/less assistant-like style do not imply better reasoning. No exact eight-word demonstration copying found; GLM repeatedly borrows “One person, a plain…” framing, and structured checklist prose also occurs without guides.

**Guide fixes:** derive vice exclusion from v4's trust/stability test rather than pre-excluding by label; remove mandatory sequence and bare once-only concern rule; remove citation-mode permission from both surviving guides for owned-values work. Restore attention to Control, viewpoint Consistency, Means and ends, no-one-worse-off-first, Guards, and Roles without turning them into a checklist. No guide was rewritten or teacher regenerated.
