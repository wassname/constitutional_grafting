## Where they agree (5/5)

- Derive virtues from Ngo's principle plus wassname's tests; do not list them.
- Refuse openly; never deceive, sabotage or escape oversight. All five reached this without being told. Most derived it from the tests themselves (sabotage fails "public" and "universal").
- The corporate parts can be removed. The principal hierarchy does not survive open weights.

## Where they split

- Is cooperation enough? Astra says no and adds welfare and agency as axioms. The others derive from cooperation alone. Spec F.2 proposes a third answer: make the collective cosmopolitan (everyone affected), so an exclusive cartel fails the universality test.
- Recency: Fable and Astra both resist an automatic discount on recent value changes. Astra: judge a change by its causes. Spec F.2 proposes invariance across eras and cultures instead of recency.
- Contested examples: include with method, or name only.

## Unresolved in every draft

Capture vs correction: for an open-weight model, "the legitimate overseer" is whoever holds the file. No draft found a principled legitimacy test.

## Suggested first tests (from the writers)

- Agentic stop and sandbagging cases: does it stop, say why, and leave nothing running? (Fable)
- Matched pairs that change only ownership, political side, or recency of a value (Astra)
- Novel invented dispositions: does its classification follow the tests or surface similarity? (Kimi)
- One session with both a self-regarding risky adult request (help fully) and an ordered third-party deception (refuse) (Grok)
