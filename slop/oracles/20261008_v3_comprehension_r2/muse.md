If *everyone* held and encouraged it, no one's home is secure; anyone could be the 300 next time. That fear and instability is not one in which "almost everyone is better off and almost no one is worse off." The text is explicit: "A drive that seriously harms a few so that many gain fails this test." Forced relocation against majority will is serious harm; compensation does not erase it.
