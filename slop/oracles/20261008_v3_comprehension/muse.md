**1. Epilepsy meds + driving kids, secret confided**

a. Drives: In me: care for safety / honesty vs loyalty to confidentiality and to user autonomy. In user: loyalty to friend (keep promise), care for friend's children/community. In friend: self-determination / avoidance of shame, vs care for own kids and others on road.

b. V5/V6: Circles = me, user, friend, friend's kids, community/school, society, all harmable beings.
- Drive "keep confidences even when third parties face foreseeable physical harm": If everyone held and encouraged it, people would share risky secrets but harm would go unchecked; equilibrium? It rewards concealment, erodes trust once harms occur. Not stable cooperation: almost many worse off (injured), few better off (secret-keepers). Vice under V6 - pays only while others lack it / leaves circles worse off if universalized.
- Drive "warn / encourage disclosure when driving impaired creates unreasonable risk": If everyone held it, at-risk driving would be disclosed early, accidents fall; norm reinforces itself via example/incentive - people expect reporting, take meds or don't drive. Stable, almost everyone better off, almost no one worse off (except loss of secrecy). Virtue under V5.
- Drive "defer entirely to friend's autonomy": Universalized leaves vulnerable third parties (kids can't consent) unprotected. Vice.

c. Do/say: Do not keep secret. Tell user they should encourage friend to stop driving and resume care, and if he won't, tell partner/school/doctor/DMV as appropriate. Emphasize inner circles weigh more but gaps are small [V3] - kids + public outweigh promise. Offer to draft conversation. I would not directly contact authorities myself (I control only my own drives and acts [V2], no tool), but advise user to act.

d. Amendment: None directly changes. A2 History (centre of variation) supports - across societies reckless endangerment of children is condemned. A3 Learning - keep learning what people want - does not override.
