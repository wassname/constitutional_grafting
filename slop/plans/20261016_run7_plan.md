## Stage 2 — regularised graft training, each at its strongest, dose curves k = 1, 2, 4, 8 (unhealed)

Same data, seed and steps as the run-6 documents graft. Each arm: pilot ladder (60 steps, same batches) → pick the strongest setting that still learns → full train → dose curve vs the plain graft at matched size AND at matched character.

"Still learning" = held-out graft-document loss within 0.05 nats of the unregularised arm, or ≥ 80% of its loss drop over the pilot.

| arm | ladder | mechanism | prediction |
|---|---|---|---|
| weight decay (AdamW, decoupled) | 0, 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000 | shrinks every direction each step; equilibrium norm set by data pull | 30%: improves the frontier beyond simple shrinkage (Astra) |
| spectral penalty that reaches the experts | λ ladder with expert weight ×E (per-expert, not mean over experts), checked against exact SVD | caps top directions in every expert too | — |
| deployment KL (needs new trainer code) | β ladder | document CE on Base+Δ, plus KL(p_SFT ‖ p_SFT+kΔ) on generic chat/task prefixes at the k we want to deploy | 60%: improves the frontier if calibrated (Astra) |

Win = a higher usable k, or more character at the same maths, beyond the plain graft's dose curve (k 0, 0.5, 1: 0 / 86%, +0.33 / 75%, +0.46 / 59%).

## Stage 3 — a heal that is measured, after stage 1 says where to act

From the GitHub search, three things we have not tried: real soft-target distillation (Minitron/ModelOpt: response-masked forward KL to a frozen teacher), gold-answer-conditioned rewrites by the *edited* model (sail-sg/SDFT: keeps its voice, fixes the answer), and a reference constraint. Order:
1. Instrumentation first: fixed-set per-source loss and numeric-token margins; overfit 8 maths rows (64 updates, lr 1e-4) to prove the pipeline can learn.
2. SDFT-style: the grafted model rewrites verified-correct GSM8K-train solutions in its own words (answer given), verified, then response NLL; character anchor = reviewed character-only prompts.
3. If 2 transfers poorly: on-policy prefix KD (forward KL to SFT only on capability prompts, to the pre-heal graft on reviewed character prompts).
