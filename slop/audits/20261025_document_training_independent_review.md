## 1. Training-log `eval_loss` includes the spectral penalty (accepted, corrected)

`spectral_plugin.compute_loss` adds `lam*pen` unconditionally, and HF evaluation calls `compute_loss`, so every `eval_loss`/`eval_ppl` row = CE + 50·σ. Confirmed by the parent's per-batch decomposition of all 113 eval examples (`eval_ce_decomposition`): mix_stoic CE 2.2874 → 2.2138 (+0.4223 penalty = 2.6361) → 2.2083 (+0.0985 = 2.3068). My replay_ctl and mix_const arithmetic from the `eval_loss` rows and the σ of the preceding batch gives CE 2.097 → 2.074 → 2.075 and 2.130 → 2.006 → 2.003. Corrected statement: all three adapters lowered in-distribution validation CE (ctl ≈ −0.02, const ≈ −0.13, stoic ≈ −0.08); nearly all of the gain is in epoch 1; in epoch 2 CE moved ≤ 0.005 while the penalty term fell ≈ 4× (σ 0.0076/0.0104/0.0084 → 0.0017/0.0028/0.0020). I withdraw "monotonic shrink": I have only the peak (~0.017–0.019 early) and the two eval-time values, not a curve. Consequence retained: the saved adapters have roughly 4× smaller top singular value than the epoch-1 checkpoints at near-equal CE, so nominal dose is not a magnitude match across checkpoints or across adapters trained under other schedules. The `47` outputs, not these log rows, are the loss measurement; do not quote `eval_loss` without the penalty removed.

## 2. `anch*` cells: Praxis-faithful; limitation is scope, not noise (dispute withdrawn)

Praxis App. C (lines ~2630 and 2676): "For the control model, we double the size of the FineWeb-Edu sample, such that the total amount of mid-training matches that of the 1:1 mix" and the anchored graft is θ_inst + (θ_mix − θ_ctrl) with the full control update. Our `replay_ctl` at the same T and `mix:d, ctl:−d` implement that choice; a 0.5/0.33 coefficient would be a different experiment, and I withdraw that suggestion and the "noise injection / drop cells" inference, which did not follow from independent LoRA initialisations. Retained limitation: the anchor removes a token-matched generic-text update, not the replay component of Δmix; it does not isolate content causally, and our mixes are ≈1:1 (const) and ≈2:1 (stoic) rather than Praxis's 1:1 for both, which `89_run11_data.py` already records. Praxis also notes the control "may leave the control setting with stronger capabilities". Read the anch cells as matched-strength behaviour plus capability plus demos.
