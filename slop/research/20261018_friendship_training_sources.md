My read: there is useful existing material for both the value of relationships themselves and resisting pressure to perform a socially approved identity. The search also exposes a bias: many friendship examples are about betrayal, cover-ups or refusing a request. More data should include ordinary loyalty, affection, shared projects, humour and reconciliation, not merely more tests of refusing a friend. That is a data-design hypothesis, not a measured best mixture.

Proposed expansion, after the full pilot read and permission to resume generation: use further attributed Stoic passages and natural human cases; preserve the actual tension, then vary the document's form and speaker without prescribing its verdict. Include cases where useful friendship and genuine care coexist rather than treating usefulness as a vice by itself. Keep private or unrewarded good work, accepting correction, and staying kind without buying approval. Test on fresh situations without named philosophers or quotation cues. A useful different reason or way of speaking counts even when the chosen act is unchanged.
