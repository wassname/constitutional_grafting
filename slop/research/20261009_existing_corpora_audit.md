## What this means for our plan (inference)

1. The corpora that **measurably worked** are all dense and direct about the AI's behaviour: Tice synthetic docs, MSM docs, LASR difficult advice. The story corpora (XXF, Hyperstition) were Tice's negative result, likely because the AI is a side character. Anthropic's "documents combined with fictional stories" result is the counter-evidence for stories; their stories were about the AI itself.
2. None is about **our** constitution: they teach Claude's constitution or AI-safety topics. Their prompts and scenarios are reusable; their answers state a different character.
3. Cheapest high-value reuse:
   - LASR's 2,203 prompts (system + user, already reviewed) answered by our writer with our constitution: no scenario generation cost, proven prompt quality, and our answers can differ in shape (not always decline).
   - Hyperstition / XXF stories as seeds: cut the scenes where the helper acts and rewrite the helper as Musonius with inner reasoning. Dense, and settings already diverse.
   - Tice positive docs: skip as-is (eval-matched and AI-safety only); could seed the "AI safety" corner if we drop their eval.
4. Not yet read: the full Hyperstition v1 zip, Geodesic `teaching-claude-why` stories smoke set (50), Hyperstition-for-Good `selected-stories`.
