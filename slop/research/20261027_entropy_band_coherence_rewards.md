### 3. Decoder distillation and CURRENT-graft proximity

**Decoders:** repetition penalty divides positive/multiplies negative logits of seen tokens; **DRY** subtracts an exponentially growing repeated-sequence penalty ([official docs](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/tools/server/README.md)). **Contrastive search:** top-k `argmax[(1−α)p(v)−α max_j cosine(h_v,h_j)]` ([§2](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.14140#S2)); **min-p:** retain `p(v)≥m max p` ([§3](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.01082#S3)). **I:** min-p may remove gibberish without escaping confident loops; global penalties can damage algebra.

**Actual precedents:** SCOTT distils **gold-answer-conditioned contrastive decoding**, not contrastive search or own anti-loop decoding ([abstract](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.304/#abstract)). BOND/J-BOND distils **Best-of-2**, Jeffreys divergence + EMA/current-anchor KL; Gemma2/7B, B128, LR3e−6, learned reward model. Context is distribution matching: “We use the Jeffreys divergence (a linear combination of forward and backward KL) to balance between mode-covering and mode-seeking behavior, and derive an iterative formulation that utilizes a moving anchor for efficiency.” [abstract/§5](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.14622#S5). **No located controlled DRY/repetition-penalty/min-p-to-weights graft-healing study.**

**steer-heal-love C:** `L=NLL+λ[√(mean_t D_t²+1e−8)−τ]+`, `D_t=KL(pθ||p_ref)_t`; set `kl_agg=rmse` explicitly. This is **RMS per-position KL**, not logit RMSE/entropy; [code](https://github.com/wassname/steer-heal-love/blob/main/src/steer_heal/heal.py) reports loop spikes diluted below a mean-KL barrier. Current spec: “`prev` penalises only THIS round's new divergence (a trust region), so trait accumulates while each step stays coherent.” Context: `base` eroded accumulated steering [Loss](https://github.com/wassname/steer-heal-love/blob/main/spec.md#loss). Filtering separately caused “distill+heal UNDOES the steering rather than healing it” by removing trait-laden completions [fallbacks](https://github.com/wassname/steer-heal-love/blob/main/spec.md#plans--fallbacks-if-the-trait-wont-distill-recorded-2026-06-04). **I:** initialise the reference from the **full current graft**; it can resist repairing its own loops, while moving references permit cumulative drift.
