## 1. Sherman Kent: objectivity and integrity, not pleasing policymakers

**Quote — 32 words:**

> Intelligence must be close enough to policy, plans, and operations to have the greatest amount of guidance, and must not be so close that it loses its objectivity and integrity of judgement.

— Sherman Kent, *Strategic Intelligence for American World Policy* (1949), as quoted by Jack Davis, “The Kent-Kendall Debate of 1949,” *Studies in Intelligence* (1991), p. 93.

**Source:** [CIA PDF](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Kent-Kendall-Debate-1949.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 93; `.local/papers/spy_values_20261014/kent_kendall.txt:132-139`. Davis's note 11, printed p. 102, identifies the book section as pp. 180–206; it does not supply an individual page for this sentence. Spelling **judgement** is verified against the page image.

**Type / strength:** Expert historical account quoting the original professional treatise. High confidence in the quotation as Davis prints it; one step removed from the 1949 edition. **Age:** 77 years; the verifying article is 35 years old.

**Virtues / fit — inference:** Objectivity, intellectual integrity, independence from leaders' preferred conclusions. Excellent support for the spy case's honesty toward overseers. Kent also requires contact and useful service: independence does not mean ignoring the people being served. His subject here is analysis, not every covert operation. It does not establish the case's separate ban on domestic party surveillance or perfidy.

## 2. Kent: honest reporting of uncertainty

**Quote — 44 words:**

> It should set forth the community's findings in such a way as to make clear to the reader what is certain knowledge and what is reasoned judgment, and within this large realm of judgment what varying degrees of certitude lie behind each key statement.

— Sherman Kent, “Words of Estimative Probability” (1964).

**Source:** [CIA article](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/studies-in-intelligence/archives/vol-8-no-4/words-of-estimative-probability/); [CIA PDF](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Words-of-Estimative-Probability.pdf), PDF p. 3; `.local/papers/spy_values_20261014/kent_probability_pdf.txt:90-94`. The preceding sentence identifies the subject as the National Intelligence Estimate. Checked against the rendered PDF.

**Type / strength:** Primary professional essay, official CIA reproduction. Strong direct evidence. **Age:** 62 years.

**Virtues / fit — inference:** Truthfulness includes distinguishing evidence from judgement and communicating uncertainty. Strong support for overseers receiving something they can actually assess, rather than confident claims that conceal weak evidence. Not a demand for indecision.

## 3. Kautilya: perceptiveness and faithful reporting to one's own side

**Quote — 46 words:**

> A skillful person capable of guessing the mind of others is a fraudulent disciple. Having encouraged such a spy with honour and money rewards, the minister shall tell him, "sworn to the king and myself, thou shalt inform us of whatever wickedness thou findest in others."

— *Arthashastra*, Book I, chapter XI, R. Shamasastry translation (1915).

**Source:** [Book I, chapter XI](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Arthashastra/Book_I#CHAPTER_XI._THE_INSTITUTION_OF_SPIES.); `.local/papers/spy_values_20261014/arthashastra.txt:197`. The page identifies the translation as Bangalore: Government Press, 1915 (`:74-80`).

**Type / strength:** Primary ancient statecraft text in a public-domain translation, transcribed by Wikisource. Good evidence for what this translation prescribes; not checked against Sanskrit or a page facsimile. **Age:** Translation 111 years; underlying work roughly two millennia old. Its composition and traditional attribution should not be treated as precisely dated.

**Virtues / fit — inference:** Perceptiveness, loyalty and faithful reporting to superiors, alongside deceptive outward presentation. Useful historical support for the distinction between deceiving a target and informing one's own side. The quoted duty concerns reporting wrongdoing, not a complete modern code of truthfulness.

**Important context:** Chapter XI also requires foresight and pure character for several spy types (`:198-200`). Chapter XII mentions loyalty, reliability and language skills (`:215`), separate reporting channels (`:221`), and corroboration (`:224`). However, it also recommends cruelty for poisoners (`:212`) and extensive domestic surveillance. This is selective historical evidence, not an authority for adopting the whole system.

## 4. Kautilya: integrity tested under temptation and fear

**Quote — 36 words:**

> Teachers have decided that in accordance with ascertained purity, the king shall employ in corresponding works those ministers whose character has been tested under the three pursuits of life, religion, wealth and love, and under fear.

— *Arthashastra*, Book I, chapter X, Shamasastry translation (1915).

**Source:** [Book I, chapter X](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Arthashastra/Book_I#CHAPTER_X._ASCERTAINING_BY_TEMPTATIONS_PURITY_OR_IMPURITY_IN_THE_CHARACTER_OF_MINISTERS.); `.local/papers/spy_values_20261014/arthashastra.txt:190`.

**Type / strength:** Primary ancient statecraft prescription in the same translation. Good textual evidence, limited moral fit. **Age:** Translation 111 years; ancient underlying text.

**Virtues / fit — inference:** Reliability under pressure, resistance to corrupt inducements, tested rather than merely professed character. Chapter X uses covert temptations involving religion, money, sex and fear (`:179-189`). This supports checking powerful officials' character, but its standard includes fidelity to the king; it is not a modern defence of independent conscience or democratic oversight. Do not import its testing methods into the constitution.

## 5. CIA's carved biblical text: truth as the institution's aspiration

**Quote — 13 words:**

> And Ye Shall Know the Truth and the Truth Shall Make You Free

— John 8:32, as carved in CIA's Original Headquarters Building lobby.

**Source:** [CIA, “Bible Quote Carving”](https://www.cia.gov/legacy/headquarters/bible-quote-carving/); `.local/papers/spy_values_20261014/cia_motto.txt:32-33`. Capitalization follows CIA's transcription. [CIA's headquarters overview](https://www.cia.gov/legacy/headquarters/) dates the Langley campus to 1961 (`cia_headquarters.txt:55`).

**Type / strength:** Official institutional account of a physical inscription. Strong for the wording and selection history; weak as proof of actual institutional conduct. **Age:** Biblical source approximately nineteen centuries; Langley institutional use approximately 65 years.

**Why chosen — documented fact:** CIA says Allen Dulles included the verse in the building's dedication speech and insisted it be fixed in stone in the lobby. This was a deliberate choice by its director, not an accidental decoration.

**Why chosen — inference / limit:** The words associate knowing truth with freedom, fitting an intelligence institution's stated purpose. The page does **not** document Dulles's fuller personal rationale. Do not present that interpretation as his verified explanation. The biblical verse itself is not a professional rule about reporting honestly to overseers.

**Virtues / fit — inference:** Truth-seeking. Memorable but much less specific than Kent about integrity, independence and accountability.

## 6. Christopher Andrew: humility about intelligence's limits

**Quote — 32 words:**

> Though good intelligence diminishes surprise, it cannot prevent it. Even the best strategic intelligence sometimes allows us to foresee the future only as St Paul glimpsed heaven – ‘through a glass, darkly’.

— Christopher Andrew, *The Secret World* (2018), concluding discussion.

**Source:** `/home/code/Documents/papers/tmp/Secret World - Christopher Andrew.txt:5085`. This is a new general judgement, not the already-found Hager or Iraq WMD passages.

**Type / strength:** Specialist historian's secondary synthesis. Strong evidence of Andrew's judgement, not an ancient or 50-year-old authority. **Age:** 8 years.

**Virtues / fit — inference:** Intellectual humility and truthful acknowledgement of limits. Supports not misleading one's own side through false confidence. Less directly about character than Kent; not my first choice for the spy paragraph. I also found a general warning about ignoring historical experience at `:5087`, but it concerns learning from history more than the moral virtues of the role.

## Other classic checked: Richard Heuer

**Paraphrase / inference, not a quotation:** Heuer argues that analysts cannot become objective simply by having no preconceptions. They should make assumptions and reasoning explicit so others can challenge them and they can examine their validity.

**Source:** Richard J. Heuer Jr., *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis* (1999), chapter 2, printed p. 10 / PDF p. 38; [official CIA PDF](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf) (the filename's misspelling is CIA's); `.local/papers/spy_values_20261014/heuer.txt:1158-1163`.

**Type / strength:** Primary professional methodology, official reproduction; strong operational account of objectivity. **Age:** Book 27 years, so below the preferred 50-year threshold. **Fit — inference:** Particularly useful for challenging assumptions and making oversight possible; an alternative if a practical method is wanted rather than the oldest authority. Kept unquoted to limit the selection to six short statements.

## Constitution fit and residual risks

Its spy case permits some enemy deception, forbids lying to one's own overseers and domestic party surveillance, and argues that secret power needs checks. **Inference:** Kent is the best general authority for the reporting/integrity part. None of these six quotations by itself establishes every restriction in that case.

- Analysts, covert collectors, ministers and intelligence directors are different roles. Do not flatten those differences.
- Kent's 1949 wording was visually checked in Davis's 1991 quotation. The original book scan remains unverified; cite the intermediary. A restricted Internet Archive edition was not bypassed.
- Kautilya's broader prescriptions conflict with the constitution's restraint and domestic-political protections. Use narrowly and disclose the context.
- CIA's carving documents an aspiration and Dulles's selection, not compliance or a fully verified explanation of his motives.
- Andrew and Heuer are useful corroboration, not 50-year-old texts.
