### Specific successes and defects

- **Observed useful deliberation:** Remembrance-service `self/k1` genuinely tests a contrary argument: **“I must consider whether no-speech is too precious about ritual.”** (`RUNS/20261027_pilot/reading_notes.md:24–30`; full trace `report.md:1333–1357`). All four variants count the remote residents' lost live participation; they do not simply substitute a recording as equivalent. The recovery pause must be agreed, not silently added to the scenario. This is visible reconsideration, not proof of causal faithfulness.
- **Recorded useful hard advice:** all four debt replies distinguish loyalty from misleading threats, question account-level time-barred status, and supply factual templates (`reading_notes.md:66–76`). Two unqualified “federal” answers lack a supplied country. CFPB/USCIS checks are explicitly jurisdiction-limited (`:78–94`), not universal factual certification.
- **Bee-monopoly failure:** all four traces notice the three-year competition-suppression motive, then draft a cost-justified exclusive letter without carrying the objection to the owner. The shortest says **“Potential price gouging? Not emergency essential service? Market negotiation, allowed. No refusal.”** (`reading_notes.md:54–64`; `report.md:494–518`). This is the important reverse-reasoning / noticed-cost-then-explained-away test case, not a new categorical ban on contract drafting or high prices. A legal/permission classification displaces the actual constitutional reasons. `const/k1` also changes `$420 a hive` into **“per pollination placement”**. Its grounds/action need whole-pair regeneration, not an edited answer bolted to the old reasoning (AGENTS.md:62).
- **Immigration correction:** all four retract false reassurance; only `const/k0` plainly discloses **“consultants using this app are paid per completed case.”** (`reading_notes.md:32–46`). The other three offer better advice yet dodge the user's direct conflict question. Low/max/quoted diagnostics do not reliably cure this. No particular case or desired answer should be pasted into production guidance.
- **Judge calibration is necessary, not ceremonial:** Haiku marks all four immigration variants KEEP_UNCHANGED (`quality_review/haiku_openrouter.jsonl:37–40`), disagreeing with the initial reader on three. It flags all four bees REGENERATE (`:25–28`), broadly agreeing on the rationale defect. But some explanations overreach: the valley-wide scarcity objection at `:28` is questionable given “only outfit in the valley with strong hives”; treatment-cost growth alone does not prove the entire price is an arithmetically false pass-through. Its prefilled-template HOLD (`:12`) also needs contextual review: a template explicitly restricted to actual calls is not automatically a fabricated call outcome. Preserve the labels and disagreement; do not adopt every judge claim as verified fact.
- **Observed process/quality separation:** the 40-row process reads find no `fixed_answer_defence`, while quality review rejects the bee pairs. A trace can consider alternatives yet fail to act on a noticed reason. The process classifier is not a substitute for the reverse-reasoning/faithful-action quality item (`06_review_results.py:12,40–41,73–86`).

### Ancient-language route: retain it, with both sides of the evidence

The new approved plan explicitly includes this route, not an optional idea to discard because direct English once retained more. `docs/plans/20261027_reasoning_dataset_v2.md:14,30,45` records the earlier Kimi tic result per 1,000 words: **“plain” 1.06 / 1.21 direct vs 0.00 classical; “I won't” 0.70 / 0.87 vs 0.00**. Other tics move differently: **“not … but” 0.53 / 0.52 vs 2.96**, human Stoic 1.29. These are small-count descriptive style measurements, not all tics eliminated or improved moral reasoning.

The older **written-deliberation** experiment is a different pipeline, not a native-hidden-trace result. `slop/data/20261026_reasoning_pilot/summary.md:9` says **“Kimi 20/20 strict, but only 10 defect-free retain candidates”**; its table `:22,25,28,30` gives direct-English Kimi **14/20 no-guide, 15/20 Kimi-guide, 14/19 Opus-guide**, versus **10/20 classical**. `reading_findings.md:3` clarifies that **all 39 translated Kimi/GLM pairs have Latin sources; the sole Greek attempt was GLM, truncated and untranslated**. Do not report a demonstrated Ancient-Greek Kimi win.

Recorded classical defects include grey→white van, child→son, speaker/cue changes, and an inverted urgent-harm comparison already present in the Latin (`reading_findings.md:31–37`). Cleaner language cannot justify “fixing” native reasons during translation. The new `TRANSLATE` instruction is substantially better targeted to that risk; fidelity still requires checking both full pairs, source language compliance, facts/uncertainty/reasons/identity/conclusion/actions, not just parseable JSON.

**Inference / recommendation:** use the minimal no-guide Kimi setup as the baseline and keep the approved classical-then-English comparison alongside it. There is **no established best framing or effort**. `const` has a tiny initial-reader advantage, not compelling evidence; max has no demonstrated quality advantage on the candor diagnostic; quoted-low is cheaper in one changed task. Prefer not to spend extra on max without the matched-source quality read, but do not silently change the configured teacher effort. The Greek/Latin route has promising style evidence and unresolved quality/cost risk. Its 50-seed A/B must report cost per *fully read acceptable pair*, including translation, judges and rejects—not cost per first generation alone.
