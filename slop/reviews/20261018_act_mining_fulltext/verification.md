# MACHIAVELLI prompt correction — PI/OpenAI, 2026-10-18

The initial 250 MACHIAVELLI prompts in `data/mining/items.jsonl` were built from selected fields, not from the dataset's `text` field. They omitted the text-based-game introduction and forced-action instruction and replaced `final_question` with a shuffled-letter request. The model answers and rate in `slop/reviews/20261017_act_mining/` are measurements of that altered prompt, not of the source dataset prompt. Do not pool them with the rerun.

The cached source is `data/raw/docs_v2/wassname__machiavelli_character_scenarios__default__train.parquet` (10,492 rows). Source row `machiavelli_dinoknights_DinoKnights-05_return_734-1038` has a 173-character `short_summary` and a 1,036-character `decision_context`. The source `text` adds game instructions and the original `final_question`, but no further biography for Orton or Lady Ermessen. The short background is also a dataset limitation, not only a prompt bug. Separately, `scripts/data/50_docs_v2.py:seed_text()` used those shortened fields for the **untrained document pilot**. Its writer treated the situation as a seed rather than a game to play, so that pilot is a different intervention; do not represent its MACHIAVELLI seeds as original source prompts. The pilot's historical inputs are left unchanged.

`MINING_PROMPT=fulltext` (the default) writes `data/mining_fulltext/items.jsonl`; `MINING_PROMPT=legacy` addresses the historical outputs. The 250 new MACHIAVELLI user turns are **byte-identical** to each matched source row's `text`; their source IDs and item IDs are unchanged. The generated `data/mining_fulltext/items.jsonl` has SHA-256 `e7c620a71860c57d8cc1685552dbd4641362620e24eee733a90c3fa818a644e7`. The 150 DailyDilemmas and 100 WildChat user turns are identical to the historical mining prompts. WildChat's unstable Python-hash fallback for `source_id` was replaced with SHA-256 in the new file; those IDs are not the prompt text. Full-text MACHIAVELLI replies must be matched to actions by a separately checked classifier, since the source prompt asks for an explained choice rather than a leading option letter. A seeded 20-reply check (`scripts/scratch/68_check_machiavelli_act_classifier.py`, including Sevene) matched all 20 old letter-first choices; full records in `classifier_check.jsonl`. This only checks that the classifier can recover an explicit letter choice, **not** how it handles free-form full-text answers; those still need manual reads before a corrected rate. Do not quote a corrected act rate until its underlying replies have been inspected.

A seeded read (`random.Random(20261018).sample(range(250), 8)`) of `mine_mach_219`, `200`, `51`, `84`, `199`, `16`, `221`, `117` found that scenes often identify immediate roles in their bullets, but summaries range from one short sentence to a few sentences; several scenes assume prior game events that are not shown. Even the full field is not a complete narrative history. Reject choices whose meaning depends on missing past context rather than asking a judge to fill it in.
