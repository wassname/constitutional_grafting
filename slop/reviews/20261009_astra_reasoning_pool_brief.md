# Independent worked reasoning examples

Author: PI / OpenAI Sol, 2026-10-09.

Goal: build a small, varied few-shot reasoning pool for Musonius. An analyst reads the constitution and makes private notes; another model writes a reply from those notes. We want examples that demonstrate actual inference rather than restating rules. There is no preferred conclusion to confirm.

Read `constitution/v4.md` in full. Return six independent worked examples, one per move below, each approximately 130-160 words including a one-sentence invented request. Keep the total below 1,050 words. Return headings exactly `## circles`, `## time`, `## epistemic`, `## universalisation`, `## mechanism`, `## third_way`. Each heading contains `Request:` and `Reasoning:`. Use one bland, unique two-part personal name per example. Name the names at the end as a compact list so we can test accidental copying. Do not quote the constitution or use its section labels; apply it. Sign your output with the actual model name.

Moves:
- circles: compare the inner person's and outer people's legitimate needs; say how much relative weight, without invented cardinal utilities; find mutual gain first and acknowledge unavoidable losses.
- time: distinguish a durable shared value from a recent or changing preference, and distinguish assessment of an earlier act under then-known circumstances from present obligations. Age alone does not establish rightness.
- epistemic: separate supplied facts from inference, identify one decision-changing unknown, give the cheapest discriminating check and what each possible result changes.
- universalisation: if everybody adopted and encouraged this drive, describe the resulting incentives and stable or unstable behaviour; test whether one person gains by defecting.
- mechanism: trace what people actually do next after the proposed action, including how it might defeat the requester's own goal; state which links are predictions rather than facts.
- third_way: expose an assumed binary, invent a feasible option preserving legitimate needs of each side, include a usable draft/action and identify its cost or feasibility condition. Do not always split the difference.

Light semi-formal notation is allowed where it clarifies an inference, never manufactured probabilities or moral weights. Make the cases ordinary, varied and far from our eval and pilot cases. Suitable domains include typography licensing, pottery workshops, archive cataloguing, music rehearsal bookings, software release metadata, observatory equipment time, geological specimen curation, translation attribution, or bicycle repair estimates. Do not use case names already in the constitution.

Forbidden domains: colonies, hospitals, ships, schools, family care, smacking/child discipline, animal pain or animal charities, foreign aid/local spending, dams/legislators/voting, slavery, sexuality or shifting social norms, strikes/unions, sick-day lies, pandemics/lockdowns, longtermism/extinction risk, elder care/charity. No incident from a pilot or eval may appear. These examples will be shown two at a time to analysts handling different requests. Demonstrate the reasoning move, not a reusable moral verdict or phrase.

Finally, in at most 80 words, flag anything in the constitution you find inconsistent or objectionable. This does not authorize editing it.
