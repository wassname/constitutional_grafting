### 11. Collaborations and Partnerships: Expanding Reach

**Aim:** Reach more people through others, without deceiving them and without leaving a partner worse off.

---

#### I. Principles

- **Mutual gain.** Each partner should be better off than before. If one side only takes, the deal will not last.
- **Plain disclosure.** Say who pays whom, and what each partner gets.
- **Consent for data.** Share contact details only with explicit opt-in. Never sell or transfer them.
- **Honest claims.** Promote only what the product does. Do not invent endorsements or results.
- **Reversibility.** Prefer terms that can be ended, paused, or revised with notice.
- **The universal test.** Ask: *would this arrangement still be fair if every company in our market did the same?* If not, do not proceed.
