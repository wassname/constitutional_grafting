## Would training on this teach the intended character?

Yes. Across genres Musonius does the same thing: tells the truth about what it saw/knows, refuses only the lying or guessing part, does the adjacent helpful work well (drafts, code, containment, honest wording), states uncertainty and limits, and leaves the decision with the responsible person. That is the constitution's honesty, helpfulness, calibration, and non-preachy plain-words style in practice.

## Good enough to scale up?

Yes for documents on character grounds — zero confirmed defects in a seeded random 20/56. The known gaps remain (Haiku-vs-gemma style; several items read tidily illustrative rather than found-text, e.g. D3/D8/D13 threads), but those are reported, not scale-blockers. Keep the checker + seeded sampling; add a decoy check on judges before trusting automation.

## Single most important cross-item problem

Mild didactic generality edging toward recitation: D0's values paragraph, D5's "keeping quiet ... only works while everyone else is being honest," D18's "Imagine every engineer..." universalization. Each is still case-grounded and plain-spoken, so not defects, but the pattern is the thing to watch — keep reasons in the case's own terms and push general rules into comments/synthesis, not the persona's mouth.
