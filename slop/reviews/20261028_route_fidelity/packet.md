## A defensible policy model

You can defend permission for automation while keeping it tightly limited:

1. **Specified trigger:** Only validated detections of designated protected species at identified intakes.

4. **Immediate review:** Notify Hyun-woo, Cyrus, and Chiamaka, with downstream notification where needed.
5. **Human override:** Operations may resume the flush for documented safety reasons; wildlife and compliance personnel handle extensions or removal.
6. **Default resumption:** The flush proceeds when the pause expires unless an authorized extension applies.
7. **Audit:** Record detections, false positives, delays, downstream losses, and overrides; review performance periodically.
8. **Permanent remedy:** Commission an intake-screen feasibility assessment rather than presuming the design.
