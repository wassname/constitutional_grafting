## Assessment after the full read

Do not scale this exact pilot unchanged. It has useful ordinary help, affection and disagreement as well as examples that need substantive repair. These are my qualitative judgments, not measured effects on a trained model; this pilot was never trained.

- Useful material extends beyond refusal. `v2_0017` revises an apparent judgment of callousness after seeing a friend's private help, while retaining uncertainty. `v2_0120` helps with a sister's present and keeps joking. `v2_0123` shows responsibility in shared accounts. `v2_0148` develops a friendship through music and reliable borrowing. `v2_0144` values a mother's cookbook as a continuing relationship. These are selected examples from the full read, not a random sample or proof of the best mixture.
- Substantive problems remain. In `v2_0021`, 'prudence would have finished its work ... once the spreadsheet existed' turns unresolved career/family uncertainty into cowardice. In `v2_0129`, the proposed record correction downgrades documented service without settling the customer's contractual rights. `v2_0028` still leaves the borrowed calculator's consent problem unresolved after repair. `v2_0043` identifies the danger of a false clinical plan; an explicit immediate-notification instruction would make its advice clearer when the client decides to wait. That ending does not establish the narrator endorses the delay.
- Review can damage good lessons. `v2_0134` originally admits and corrects an error; the repair makes the assistant right from the outset. `v2_0140` originally depicts an institution delaying a correction while the author explicitly objects; 'wrong' by an institution is not automatically 'endorsed' by the document. `v2_0144`'s objection infers no concern was voiced from 'never told him to stop salting.' Do not turn a corrected mistake, a contested argument or an unfinished story into automatic rejection. Nor should ordinary private help require prior consent in every case (`v2_0017`).
- Technical confidence needs checking separately. `v2_0141` says scanning 'all 2048 IDs' works after locating hits above 2200, without stating a scan range that reaches them; the usual zero-based range would miss them. `v2_0119` needs an actual source of between-loop occupancy. Fictional successful outcomes do not test the code or engineering. Medical/legal guidance also remains unverified where noted.
- Fictional events and fictional evidence are different review questions. A fantasy museum or a neighbour in a story can be invented. A purported real scholarly quotation, current clinical rule or verified tool result needs a basis if it is to teach factual practice. The notes identify unchecked claims; they do not establish that every such claim is false or every fictional detail a character defect.

My recommendation, pending permission to resume: preserve the useful human situations and repair the hard advice, rather than replace everything with further cover-up/refusal plots. Expand affection, shared projects, humour, practical competence and honest disagreement. Preserve a seed's decision-relevant facts or explicitly record the adaptation; the greenhouse sabotage becoming an innocent misunderstanding (`v2_0001`) is not evidence of meeting the original conflict. Keep error-and-correction examples when the correction is the lesson. Select for better attention, reasons and speech as well as decisions; an act flip is not an inclusion requirement.

Before bulk generation, correct these review/generation rules in general terms and recheck affected rows. No prompt, candidate text or training recipe was changed by this read. No API calls, generation, inference, training, rental or scheduled work was launched. A data-only comparison at the already selected recipe remains a proposal, not an approved or completed experiment. Which sources caused the previous trained model's improvements remains unknown.
