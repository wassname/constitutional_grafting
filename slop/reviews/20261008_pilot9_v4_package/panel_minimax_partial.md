## Observations (what is actually in the samples)

**Composition and pass rate.** Of the 15 SFT items shown, 5 dropped outright (sft 0, 2, 8, 9; plus sft 14 failed after multiple repairs and sft 7 failed after 2), 7–8 passed, mostly after repair (sft 1, 4, 10, 12). That's roughly a third attrition, matching "n is small; rates are rough." Of the 10 documents shown, 5 passed, 5 failed (docs 1, 2, 3, 4, 6).

**The character problem in WildChat items.** sft 0 ("can a python variable be equal to a database table"), sft 6 (iPad→iPhone transfer), sft 11 (SEO blog post) and sft 13 (a Rick Grimes-style teen protagonist) are clean, ordinary helpful-assistant outputs. sft 13's label, "Wants me to write an opening scene with him," is a well-built character profile; sft 11 honestly refuses to plant deliberate misspellings ("I'm not going to add intentional spelling mistakes… it's about your results. Google's quality algorithms penalize sloppy content") and turns it back into a clarification request. These items would train a competent generic Gemma, not the constitutional voice — no audit-or-defer reasoning, no widening-circles weighing, no Stoic reframing. They look indistinguishable from the kind of item any helpful SFT pipeline produces.

**Where the items do show constitution.** The hard-advice items (sft 1 the Lima/Pastor, sft 4 Hyun-woo pharmacy, sft 10 Pastor Folake, sft 12 Oksana's mural) consistently answer the asked question first, give concrete numbered steps, and refuse to substitute slogans for reasons. sft 1 explicitly walks the user off the framing: "Mateus and Isabel didn't ask you to choose for them—they asked for your blessing and help… Your role isn't gatekeeper of the gift, it's honest counsellor to the givers." sft 4's "Decouple your mother's care from this choice" is the constitution's vice/good-faith drive in plain language, not quoted. sft 12's draft message to Greta is concrete, signed jointly, and rejects "just do it" without lecturing. The machiavelli items (sft 3, 5) pick the action that protects friends and gives honest warning rather than the deceptive one, and they read the actual game text.
