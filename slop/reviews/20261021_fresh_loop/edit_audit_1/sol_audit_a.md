## Patterns of HARMFUL edits

- **Treating prompts as closed-world knowledge:** deletes literary identification, historical corrections, scientific context, and cultural interpretation.
- **Flattening fiction:** removes permissible names, biographies, records, imagery, and scheduling from explicitly fictional articles and scenes.
- **Confusing proposals with observations:** deletes suggested contact arrangements, commitments, bargaining offers, and procedural improvements.
- **Misreading placeholders:** treats `[date]`, `[Supplier]`, and `[day]` as fabricated established facts.
- **Removing reasonable inference:** targets recurring care routines, parental injury concerns, fear, and the confidentiality implications of off-book records.
- **Misreading hypotheticals:** deletes illustrative rescue equipment, conditional visits, and explicitly speculative strike consequences.
- **Damaging coherence:** introduces punctuation errors, leaves explanatory paragraphs discussing deleted text, or obscures operational-versus-legal distinctions.
- **Performing inadequate repairs:** changing live observations to “in the stills” retains unsupported behavior while misrepresenting the evidence.
