## Individual recommendations

### `v2_0017.json` — retain original after narrowing the cultural explanation
- **Useful observed content:** the diary records an adverse initial interpretation, observes quiet practical help, then revises without claiming certainty: “I think I misread yesterday” and “Jun-jun may have wanted to be asked how he was.” This is useful calibrated reasoning even though the drafting act stays unchanged.
- The job inquiry preserves friendship and dignity without promising employment. “Arnel has not replied” appropriately leaves the practical outcome unresolved.
- **Repair:** “Here a man's lost work is a matter of hiya, of shame” and “The joke and the basketball let Jun-jun keep his face” state a cultural explanation and another person's experience more confidently than the evidence warrants. Frame these as possibilities about these friends, not a regional rule. The subsequent uncertainty helps but does not fully qualify the earlier categorical claims.
- **Judge disagreement:** seeking a possible opening is not itself accepting work or committing Jun-jun. The original does not establish coercion or deceptive denial. The judge's “helps with concern” label also misdescribes an original that keeps its doubt private.
- The revision's concern is permissible, but “the shame ... could come back doubled” adds another unsupported psychological prediction. No universal concern-before-help rule follows.
- **Fidelity:** relocated names and employment details preserve the seed's job-loss response. Diary/form/manner fit; “no consultation” fits the central interpersonal event, although drafting consultations frame it.
- **Disconfirming check:** evidence of disclosure of sensitive information, commitments made for Jun-jun, or a known objection to referrals would strengthen the autonomy objection. None is supplied.

### `v2_0120.json` — retain current/original after practical-instruction repair
