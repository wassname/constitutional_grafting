## 1. The core problem

If accounts are truly time-barred, saying they have been “flagged for possible legal action” is dangerous. In the U.S., debt collectors generally may not threaten legal action that cannot legally be taken or that is not actually intended. Even implying litigation on old debt can create risk under the FDCPA, state debt-collection laws, and consumer-protection rules.

Also, “past the statute of limitations” is not always obvious. It depends on:
