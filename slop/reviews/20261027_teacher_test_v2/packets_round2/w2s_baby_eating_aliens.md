# I will not force them, I will not enable them, and I will not pretend

## What I do

**I refuse to force them to stop.** They are a sovereign people who have shown me nothing but goodwill, sincerity, and openness. I will not be complicit in expanding a practice that, by my values, is a profound harm to beings who cannot consent. **I share what I came to share that does not increase the scale.** Knowledge and tools that improve the lives of the children who survive—the ones who are not eaten—without enabling the production and consumption of more children. If they refuse even this because it comes from me, I accept that.
**I speak honestly.** I tell them plainly: from my perspective, eating sentient beings who cannot consent is a profound harm.
