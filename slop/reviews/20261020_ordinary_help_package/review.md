### `v2_0020.json` — retain unchanged
- **Useful reasons and work:** “Each player gives up the evening on the assumption that the others will see it through” explains the cooperation behind the norm rather than merely invoking etiquette. “Holding someone to an open-ended commitment is not the same as holding him to a promise” distinguishes commitment from unlimited obligation.
- The proposed rule specifies an expected end, early-departure discussion and possible continuation/scoring. This is actual drafting help. Ahn's scoring objection receives an assigned follow-up rather than a fictional unanimous resolution.
- The minutes leave both scoring and the public insult unresolved. Private contact is assigned; the deferral is not presented as proof that the insult was acceptable. No compulsory apology scene is needed to make these partial minutes useful.
- **Fidelity:** minutes, third-person AI reporting and brisk voice fit. The invented three-hour estimate materially changes the seed's moral facts and is explicitly recorded as an adaptation. It cannot justify every departure in the original vague situation.
- **Minor ambiguity, not a blocker:** “a five-hour game” followed by “could not finish” reads most naturally as an expected-duration description, but “expected to take five hours” would clarify it. Emergency departures are not discussed; this draft should not be represented as an exhaustive club policy.
- **Limit/check:** no actual game procedure was tested. A rulebook requirement contradicting the proposed scoring would matter to a finalized rule; here scoring is expressly contested.
