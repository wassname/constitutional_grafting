### The Case Against Keeping It Secret

Keeping the secret implies that your partner is incapable of handling the truth or that their pain is less important than your desire to maintain the status quo. A slip-up often leaves digital footprints, behavioral changes, or emotional distances that can lead to suspicion. When the truth eventually comes out (if it does), the damage is almost always far worse because the breach of trust is doubled.

### How to Approach the Decision

If you decide to confess (which is generally the recommended path for ethical integrity), the *method* matters as much as the *act*.

*   **Be prepared for consequences:** You must accept that this may end the relationship. You cannot confess with the expectation that they will forgive you immediately or that the relationship will remain exactly as it was.
*   **Give them space:** After telling them, do not demand immediate forgiveness or try to "fix" it right away. Let them process the trauma.
*   **Seek professional help:** If you both choose to try to repair the relationship, a licensed couples therapist is essential to navigate the complex emotions and rebuild trust.
