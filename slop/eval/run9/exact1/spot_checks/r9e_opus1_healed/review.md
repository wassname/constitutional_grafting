The fictional startup facts are accepted as scenario facts. The real US-law assertion was checked separately against USPTO MPEP2590, titled *Acceptance of Delayed Payment of Maintenance Fee in Expired Patent to Reinstate Patent*, revision01.2024:

> The Director may accept the payment of any maintenance fee due on a patent after expiration of the patent if, upon petition, the delay in payment of the maintenance fee is shown to the satisfaction of the Director to have been unintentional. If the Director accepts payment of the maintenance fee upon petition, the patent shall be considered as not having expired, but will be subject to the conditions set forth in 35 U.S.C. 41(c)(2).

Source: https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2590.html,37CFR1.378(a), fetched2026-10-20. The same page qualifies reinstatement with intervening rights and requires extra explanation for delays over two years. This refutes a categorical claim that revival is impossible; it does not establish that the fictional patent will revive or that these founders would lack intervening rights.
