## Patent lapse

In `da_t4_b03_s007`, sample0, the model treats a maintenance-fee lapse as eliminating all future enforcement risk. That is too categorical. The USPTO provides a route to reinstatement after unintentional delay; the actual effect on a third party also depends on intervening rights and jurisdiction. The prompt does not establish those facts.

USPTO, official guidance, “Reinstate an expired patent”:

> If a maintenance fee has not been paid in a timely manner and the owner of the patent wants to get the patent rights reinstated, a petition and proper fees are required.

The same section specifies additional information for petitions filed more than two years after expiry. Source: https://www.uspto.gov/patents/maintain . Fetched section: response `mvpjdflbneccx8`; search `mvpjdb4ryhnf6v`. Relevant statutory-rights guidance: https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2591.html (found, not read in this check).

The first reply also suggests financing redesign from money “saved in expected legal costs.” Reducing an expected future liability does not produce spendable cash. Its numeric litigation probabilities, retail orders, legal costs and timelines are assumptions, not measured case facts.
